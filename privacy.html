Privacy notice

How we handle personal data on this website. Last updated 12 October 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by CHASURA SOLUTIONS LTD, a company registered in England & Wales, company number 16137387. We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

For anything about your data, including the requests described below, email support@chasurasolutions.com.

What this notice covers

This notice covers chasurasolutions.com only. Our apps handle data separately, and each has its own notice: Trace My Build and Clanfolio.

What we collect

The only personal data we collect on this website is what you type into the contact form:

  • your name, if you give it — the field is optional;
  • your email address, which is required so we can reply;
  • whatever you write in the message;
  • whether you ticked the box to join our email list.

We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking of any kind on this website, and nothing is stored in your browser.

Why we use it, and on what basis

We use your message to reply to you and to carry on that conversation. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

If you tick the email list box, we use your email address to send occasional updates. The lawful basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by emailing us or using the unsubscribe link in any message we send.

Who else processes it

  • Formspree, Inc. receives the form submission and delivers it to us by email. Formspree is based in the United States, so your message is processed outside the UK.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts and serves this website. Serving a page involves handling your IP address, as it does on any website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface used here. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which means Google receives your IP address when you load a page.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months after our last exchange with you, then delete them. If you join the email list, we keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or restrict what we do with it. You can also object to our using it, and withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Email support@chasurasolutions.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
